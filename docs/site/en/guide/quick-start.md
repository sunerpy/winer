# Quick start

## 1. Start winer and connect

Sign in to the League client first, then open winer. The status bar at the bottom of the window
shows **Connected** and the region, and the overview shows your summoner, rank and recent form.

The Tencent client is started with administrator rights by WeGame, and a program without them cannot
read it. winer notices and asks to restart as administrator: choose **Yes** in the User Account
Control prompt. If you cancelled it, **Restart as administrator** in the window asks again.

Once connected, winer sets up the Pengu Loader it ships and the client plugin by itself. The first
activation of Pengu Loader needs administrator rights once: when winer is not running as
administrator, it asks to restart that way; choose **Yes**. See
[In-client](/en/guide/client#the-first-activation-needs-administrator-rights).

## 2. Enter champ select

In champ select, the **Live game** page lists every player on your team: rank, win rate and KDA over
the last 20 games, the champion and line of each recent game, and a tier, a title and a quip from
their recent form. On a map with two sides the heading says whether your team is blue or red. Choose
any player to open their full history.

Below the board, the **Callout** is a chat message ranked by tier: **Send to team** posts it to your
team, **Only me** shows it in your own chat only. To send it every game, turn on **Send in champ
select automatically** in **Automation › Callout**.

## 3. Look up a history

The **History** page opens on your own games. Type `name#tag` to look up anyone. Every game marks its
MVP, SVP and feats, and opens into the game's full scoreboard.

## 4. Turn on the automation you want

Each item on the **Automation** page has its own switch and its own modes, for example banning
automatically in ranked games only. **By mode** at the top tells you what will actually happen in one
mode.

## 5. Teammates' form inside the client

With the client plugin set up, the client's own champ select shows a line under each teammate's
name: tier and title, rank, recent win rate and KDA. To turn it off, choose **Turn off the in-client
features** on the **In-client** page. See [In-client](/en/guide/client).
